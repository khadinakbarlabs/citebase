Questions

Where is my data kept?

In a store called citebase-projects in your own Apify account. Nothing is kept on our side.

What does it cost?

Data checks are paid on your own Apify account, and your assistant asks you first every time. Ask it what a specific check costs and it tells you before running anything.

Do I need an Apify account?

Yes. Citebase runs on Apify, and the dashboard signs you in with it.
