Before you start

Common questions.

Where is my data kept?

Your project history stays in your own Apify account. Citebase saves private actions and outreach drafts in a separate, restricted store in that account.

What does it cost?

The guided tour uses fictional projects and spends no credits. In your own workspace, Apify meters platform usage. Before a paid AI check, you see its data charge cap. Account shows live monthly usage and your spending limit. You can also save the prepaid balance you see in Apify Billing.

How can I verify a result?

Each saved site audit includes its date, pages checked and findings. AI rounds show the questions and engines included. Connect your assistant to the same Apify account to read the saved result, or open a scheduled run in Apify to inspect its outcome.

Do I need an Apify account?

Anyone can explore the sample projects. To check your own site and save work, sign in with Apify. You can use the dashboard on its own or connect an assistant.
