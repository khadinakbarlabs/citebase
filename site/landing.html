A loop, not a one-off report

  1. 1

    Check the site

    Can AI crawlers read your pages? Titles, structure, schema, speed and blocked bots, scored with a short fix list.

  2. 2

    Ask the real questions

    20+ buying questions, frozen so every round is comparable. Each engine is asked more than once, because answers change.

  3. 3

    Fix one thing

    The page to write, the site that keeps getting cited instead of you, the bot you are blocking. One step at a time.

  4. 4

    Measure the change

    Re-check monthly. Citebase shows the move with a range, so a lucky answer is never sold to you as progress.

What you get

Recommendation share

The share of buying questions where an AI actually recommends you, not just mentions you. Brand-name questions are kept apart so they cannot flatter the number.

Citation gap

The sites AI cites for your rivals while leaving you out. Your outreach list, ranked.

Site health

A crawl that scores technical, on-page and AI-search readiness, and remembers what you fixed.

Real demand

Paste your Search Console export: see the questions people type and the pages you should write.

AI bot visits

Paste a server log: see which AI bots read your site and which ones you are turning away.

Memory across chats

Every project, round and to-do is saved in a store in your own Apify account. Open a new chat next month and your assistant picks up where you left off.

Spend only on yes

Every paid check is named first and capped. Reading history and planning never run one.

Two ways to use it

Same tools, same saved projects. Pick the one that fits your assistant.

For Claude Code

Plugin from GitHub

Installs the playbooks, specialist agents and the MCP connector in one go. The code is public on GitHub, so you can read every line.

claude plugin marketplace add khadinakbarlabs/citebase
claude plugin install citebase@citebase

Then type /citebase:start and give it your website.

Questions

Do I need an Apify account?

Yes. Citebase runs on Apify, and your projects are saved in your own Apify account. Signing up at apify.com takes a minute.

What does it cost?

Data checks (AI answers, crawls, keyword data) are paid on your own Apify account, and your assistant asks you first every time. Ask it what a specific check costs and it will tell you before running anything.

Which engines does it check?

ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude. Start with ChatGPT and Google; add the rest when you need them.

Does Citebase keep my data?

No. Saved projects live in a store called citebase-projects in your own Apify account. You can read, export or delete it in Apify Console.

Can it set up monthly re-checks?

It plans them and tells you exactly how to add the schedule in Apify Console, with a spending cap on every run. You stay in control of the switch.
