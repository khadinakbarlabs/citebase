Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Citebase is a dashboard that shows how a website appears in AI answers and in Google Search. It is run by Khadin Akbar ("we"). This page explains what Citebase collects, why, where it is kept and how you can remove it. Questions: hello@khadinakbar.com.

How you sign in

You sign in with your Apify account. Citebase receives a sign-in key from Apify and keeps it in an encrypted, secure cookie in your browser. We do not see or store your Apify password. Your projects, reports and settings are saved in storage inside your own Apify account, not on our servers. You can sign out at any time, and you can remove Citebase's access in Apify under API & Integrations.

What Citebase collects

  • Project details you enter: website address, brand name, competitors, search terms and questions to check.
  • Public information: public web pages, search results and AI answers about the topics you choose.
  • Google data, only if you connect Google (see below).
  • Basic technical logs kept by our hosting provider (Vercel) for security and error fixing, such as time of request and error messages.

We do not use advertising trackers or sell any data.

Google data

Connecting Google is optional. You choose each connection separately, and each asks only for what it needs:

  • Google Search Console (read only): clicks, impressions, positions, search terms, pages, countries and devices for the website you pick. Used to show your Search report and to help the assistant suggest fixes.
  • Google Analytics (read only): visits by source and landing page for the property you pick. Used to show your traffic report, including visits that came from AI assistants.
  • Gmail (send only): used only to send an outreach email that you have written or approved in Citebase, one at a time. Citebase cannot read, search or delete your email.

Your Google access key is locked with strong encryption (AES-256) and saved in a restricted storage area inside your own Apify account. Reports made from Google data are saved there too. Google data is never shown to other users, never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI models.

Citebase's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. People do not read your Google data, unless you ask us to for support, it is needed for security, or the law requires it.

Who else handles data

  • Apify stores your projects and runs the checks.
  • Vercel hosts this website.
  • Google provides the data you connect.
  • Your own AI assistant: if you add Citebase to an assistant you use (for example Claude or ChatGPT), that assistant can read your project reports when you ask it to. You choose and control that connection.
  • Data providers we pay to fetch public search results and AI answers receive only the public search terms and website addresses needed for each check, never your Google data.

Keeping and deleting data

Your data stays in your Apify account for as long as you keep it there. To remove it:

  • Click Disconnect on the Connections page to delete a saved Google key straight away.
  • Remove Citebase from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the project's storage in your Apify account, or email us and we will help delete it within 30 days.

Children

Citebase is a business tool and is not meant for anyone under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top. Big changes will also be shown in the dashboard.
