# Platform presence (white-hat)

Talk like `../../references/plain-language.md`.

## Important
If the user asks for **parasite SEO** (publishing on rented subfolders of unrelated high-DR sites, PBNs, or doorway placements), **refuse**. Explain the spam risk and offer this skill plus `press-release`, `link-building`, and `entity-seo` instead. Citebase never executes black-hat placements.

## Goal
Earn mentions and profiles on **real platforms you control or are invited to**, so Google and AI assistants can verify the entity map.

## Web connector mode
Deliver the platform checklist and bios in chat. Optional live research on who AI already cites: `quote_cost` then `call-actor` on `khadinakbar/*` only (often after an `ai_check` / `ai-search-visibility` pass). Draft profile copy may go through `check_ai_content` if it sounds robotic. Never create accounts or submit listings without an explicit yes per site.

## Workflow
1. From the entity map (or a quick brand brief), list platforms that already cite competitors in AI answers (`ai-search-visibility` / cited domains).
2. Prioritise owned or authorized profiles: LinkedIn Company + founders, YouTube, GitHub, Crunchbase, Product Hunt, G2/Capterra (if reviews are earned), Medium/Dev.to for original posts, niche associations.
3. For each surface: exact bio from the statement library, link to the canonical entity URL, disclosure rules for any gifted/paid placement (`creator-outreach` / FTC).
4. Content plan: 3–5 first-hand posts that demonstrate relationships (build logs, case studies) — not spun syndication.
5. Track which platforms AI already cites; feed wins into `citation-loop`.
6. Done / Cost so far ($0 unless a quoted Apify research job) / Next.

## Rules
- No fake reviews, bought "as seen in", or undisclosed paid posts.
- Scraping/research jobs: Apify sign-in + `khadinakbar/*` only after a quote (`quote_cost` → `call-actor`).
